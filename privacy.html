Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026.

GG Advisory Board is a personal tool used by a single person with their own Google account. No one else can sign in to it.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the tool reads the owner's own Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Tasks and Contacts data, and sends emails only to the owner.

How the data is used

The data is used only to prepare the owner's own summaries and organisation. It is processed on the owner's computer and through the AI assistants the owner uses under their own accounts. It is not sold, not shared for advertising, and this tool does not use it to train AI models.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer and accounts. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
